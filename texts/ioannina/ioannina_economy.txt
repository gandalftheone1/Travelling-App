Ioannina's economy is driven by a mix of education, services, tourism, and traditional crafts. As the largest city in Epirus, it benefits from its status as a regional hub, with a strong presence of public administration, healthcare, and retail. The University of Ioannina significantly contributes to local development, while silverwork and artisan goods maintain the city’s legacy in traditional craftsmanship. Tourism and gastronomy have also become increasingly important economic pillars.
