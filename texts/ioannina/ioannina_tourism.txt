Ioannina is a captivating destination known for its scenic beauty and historical charm. Nestled by Lake Pamvotida and surrounded by mountains, it offers a serene yet culturally rich experience. Visitors can explore the old castle, the lake island with its monasteries, and museums reflecting the city’s layered past. Its proximity to Zagorohoria, Vikos Gorge, and Dodoni makes it an ideal base for excursions into the natural and historical treasures of Epirus.
