Located in northern Greece, Thessaloniki lies along the Thermaic Gulf of the Aegean Sea. It is situated between Mount Chortiatis and the sea, giving it a scenic backdrop and coastal charm. The city’s port is one of the largest in the Balkans, providing vital maritime connections. Thessaloniki’s geography makes it a strategic transportation and commercial hub for southeastern Europe, with access to road, rail, air, and sea networks. Its Mediterranean climate features hot summers and mild, wet winters.
