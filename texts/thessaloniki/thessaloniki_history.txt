Thessaloniki has a long and diverse history that spans more than 2,300 years. Founded during the Hellenistic period, it flourished under Roman rule and became one of the most important cities of the Byzantine Empire. During the Ottoman era, it served as a major trade and cultural hub, with a mix of Christian, Muslim, and Jewish communities. The city was incorporated into modern Greece in 1912. Despite enduring events like the Great Fire of 1917 and World War II, Thessaloniki has rebuilt and grown into a modern metropolis while preserving its historical heritage.
