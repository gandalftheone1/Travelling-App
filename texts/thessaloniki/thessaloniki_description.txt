Thessaloniki is the second-largest city in Greece and the capital of the Central Macedonia region. Known for its vibrant atmosphere, rich multicultural history, and lively arts scene, it serves as a major urban center in northern Greece. Founded in 315 BCE and named after the sister of Alexander the Great, Thessaloniki combines historical depth with youthful energy, thanks to its large student population and thriving cultural life. It is a city where Byzantine, Ottoman, and modern influences coexist, creating a unique and dynamic character.
