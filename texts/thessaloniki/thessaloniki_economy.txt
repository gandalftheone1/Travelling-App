Thessaloniki plays a crucial role in the economy of northern Greece. It is a key center for trade, logistics, and industry, largely thanks to its strategic location and major commercial port—the second busiest in the country. The city has a strong presence in sectors like food processing, pharmaceuticals, textiles, and manufacturing. It is also a growing hub for technology startups and innovation, supported by local universities and research institutions. In addition, tourism and cultural industries contribute significantly to the city’s economic activity.
