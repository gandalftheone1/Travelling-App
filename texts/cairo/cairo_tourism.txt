The city is a gateway to the pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx, and it offers historic sites like the Egyptian Museum and Islamic Cairo. Visitors explore souks, mosques, Coptic churches, and Nile cruises. Despite its traffic, Cairo offers unforgettable depth and discovery.Cairo sits in northern Egypt, just south of the Nile Delta, with desert stretching beyond the river’s edge. Its location on the Nile has shaped its history and agriculture. The city has a hot desert climate with long summers and mild winters.
