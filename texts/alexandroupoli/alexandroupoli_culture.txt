Alexandroupoli has a rich cultural identity that blends Greek, Balkan, and Anatolian influences. The city hosts a variety of cultural events, festivals, and exhibitions throughout the year. Local traditions, music, and cuisine reflect the diverse heritage of its population, which includes descendants of Asia Minor and Pontic Greek refugees. Museums such as the Ethnological Museum of Thrace and the Historical Museum of Alexandroupoli preserve and showcase this cultural mosaic.
