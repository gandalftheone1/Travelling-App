Nafplio has a rich and layered history dating back to antiquity, but it rose to particular prominence during the medieval and Venetian periods. It was fortified by the Franks, Venetians, and Ottomans, who left their mark with impressive castles and architecture. After Greece's independence from Ottoman rule, Nafplio became the first capital of the modern Greek state from 1828 to 1834. Its historical landmarks, such as Palamidi Fortress and Bourtzi Castle, testify to its strategic and cultural importance over the centuries.
