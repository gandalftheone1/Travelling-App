Tourism is the lifeblood of Nafplio, drawing visitors with its romantic ambiance, historic sites, and coastal beauty. Popular attractions include the Palamidi Fortress, Bourtzi Castle, the old town with its colorful alleys, and the scenic waterfront promenade. Nafplio is also a gateway to nearby archaeological treasures such as Tiryns, Epidaurus, and Mycenae. Its blend of history, culture, gastronomy, and seaside relaxation makes it an ideal destination for both Greek and international travelers.
