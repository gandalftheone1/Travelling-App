Nafplio (or Nafplion) is a picturesque seaport town in the northeastern Peloponnese and one of the most romantic destinations in Greece. Known for its elegant neoclassical architecture, charming old town, and seaside beauty, it was the first capital of modern Greece. With its scenic harbor, cobbled alleys, and vibrant squares, Nafplio offers a harmonious blend of historical depth and relaxed Mediterranean charm.
