Chania’s history spans over 5,000 years, beginning with the ancient Minoan city of Kydonia. It later became an important center during the Roman, Byzantine, Venetian, and Ottoman periods. Each era left its mark—seen in the architecture, fortifications, and diverse cultural influences that define the city today. Chania played a significant role in the Cretan resistance during World War II and continues to honor its historic identity through well-preserved monuments and museums.
