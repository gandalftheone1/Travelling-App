Sydney was founded in 1788 as a British penal colony, marking the beginning of European settlement in Australia. Originally inhabited by the Gadigal people of the Eora Nation, the area saw profound changes as colonial expansion took place. Throughout the 19th century, Sydney transitioned from a convict outpost into a bustling port city, driven by trade, immigration, and the gold rushes. The 20th century saw its transformation into a global metropolis, culminating in hosting the 2000 Summer Olympics—a defining moment in the city's modern history.
