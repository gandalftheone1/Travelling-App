Sydney is Australia's largest and most internationally recognized city, renowned for its spectacular harbor, iconic skyline, and energetic lifestyle. As the capital of New South Wales, Sydney plays a pivotal role in the country’s cultural, financial, and political identity. It boasts world-famous landmarks like the Sydney Opera House and Harbour Bridge, an expansive coastline with over 100 beaches, and a diverse population that brings global flavors to its neighborhoods. Sydney is consistently ranked among the world's most livable cities, combining natural beauty with a cosmopolitan atmosphere.
