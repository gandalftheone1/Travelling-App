Athens has a diverse and dynamic economy that plays a central role in Greece’s overall economic landscape. As the capital city, it serves as the country’s financial, commercial, and industrial hub. Key sectors include tourism, shipping, services, and real estate, with thousands of businesses and multinational companies based in the metropolitan area. The city benefits significantly from its historical landmarks and cultural heritage, attracting millions of visitors annually. In recent years, Athens has also seen growth in startups and technology sectors, contributing to its economic resilience and modernization.
