Athens, one of the world’s oldest cities, has a recorded history spanning over 3,400 years. It rose to prominence during the 5th century BCE, known as the Golden Age, when it became a powerful city-state and the birthplace of democracy. Renowned for its contributions to philosophy, arts, and science, Athens was home to great thinkers like Socrates, Plato, and Aristotle. Despite periods of decline under Roman, Byzantine, and Ottoman rule, Athens experienced a revival in the 19th century when it was declared the capital of modern Greece. Today, it stands as a vibrant metropolis that blends ancient heritage with contemporary life.
