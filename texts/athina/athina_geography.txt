Athens is located in southeastern Greece, within the Attica region, and is surrounded by mountains on three sides—Mount Hymettus, Mount Pentelicus, and Mount Parnitha—while the Saronic Gulf lies to the south. The city is built around a central basin and has expanded significantly over time to form a large metropolitan area. Its geography is characterized by hills, such as the Acropolis and Lycabettus Hill, which offer panoramic views of the cityscape. Athens enjoys a typical Mediterranean climate, with hot, dry summers and mild, wet winters, making it an appealing destination year-round. The city's proximity to both mountains and sea gives it a unique geographic identity that supports a variety of outdoor activities and contributes to its historical development as a major cultural and trade center.
