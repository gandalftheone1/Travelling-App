Irakleio has ancient roots as the port of Knossos and grew in significance during the Byzantine and Venetian periods. It became a fortified stronghold under the Venetians, who named it Candia, and was later ruled by the Ottomans. It played a key role in the Cretan struggle for independence and in World War II. Today, Irakleio is a gateway to Minoan civilization and modern Crete.
