With roots tracing back to ancient Pharai, Kalamata has a long and storied history. It played a significant role during the Greek War of Independence and was one of the first cities to be liberated in 1821. Throughout its history, it has been influenced by Byzantine, Venetian, and Ottoman rule, which is reflected in its architecture and cultural identity. The city has recovered from natural disasters like the 1986 earthquake, emerging stronger and more developed.
