Tourism in Patras is currently experiencing a dynamic phase of growth and diversification. While the city has traditionally attracted primarily domestic visitors—especially during weekends and public holidays—its appeal is expanding thanks to cultural events, infrastructure investments, and alternative tourism offerings. The renowned Patras Carnival remains a major draw, attracting approximately 100,000 visitors annually, with a significant portion coming from Athens and a large contingent of young people and students . In addition to cultural attractions, the city is enhancing its maritime tourism profile with the development of the "Paradise Mega Yacht Marina". Furthermore, Patras is promoting alternative tourism activities such as wine tasting, sailing, and paragliding, leveraging its natural and cultural assets to attract a broader range of visitors . These initiatives are positioning Patras as an emerging destination that combines cultural richness with diverse tourist experiences.
